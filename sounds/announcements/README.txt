Put your own prerecorded announcements here (.wav or .mp3).

  sounds/announcements/<Folder>/<NN Name>.wav           one file per announcement, or
  sounds/announcements/<Folder>/<NN Name>/<Language>.wav  one file per language

Language files may also be named after their folder plus a language code, e.g.
  sounds/announcements/08_after_landing/after_landing_en.mp3
which the FAP shows as "08 After landing > English".

Folders show up on the FAP Audio page and can be opened/closed.
Press "Rescan" on the FAP Config page after adding files while the sim is running.
