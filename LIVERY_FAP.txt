ToLiss FAP - per-livery content
===============================

Any livery can carry its own FAP content in a folder called "FAP" inside the livery:

  <aircraft>/liveries/<Livery name>/FAP/
      screen_off.png            image shown when the FAP screen is switched off
                                (.jpg also works; 1100 x 840 px is ideal).
                                Leave it out to get the default screen;
                                use a plain black image for "nothing".
      sounds/music/*.wav|mp3    boarding music for this livery
      sounds/announcements/...  announcements for this livery, same layout as the main set:
                                <NN Announcement>/<Language>.wav  (folders optional)

A FAP folder with only a screen_off image is fine: the general sounds are still used.

Livery sounds are added on top of the general sounds in plugins/ToLissFAP/sounds:
  - Boarding music: the list shows a LIVERY section first, then GENERAL.
  - Announcements: the livery's own announcements are listed first; the general
    ones are grouped in a "General" folder.

Audio: .wav or .mp3 (mono or stereo, any sample rate). The FAP picks up the
livery when the aircraft loads or the livery is changed; press Rescan on the
FAP Config page after adding files (or the image) while the sim is running.

Livery painters: you are welcome to ship a FAP folder with your livery.
Only include sounds you have the right to distribute.
